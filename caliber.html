01 / Context

Building a Process Where None Existed

When I joined Caliber, there was no formal process for requesting or approving creative — art requests came in ad hoc, with no consistent review before work went live. I set up a working process and became the point of contact for each line of business's Senior Marketing Director — Auto Care, Collision and Auto Glass — so that every piece of creative had a clear path from request to approval.

Step 1

Request

Marketing Directors submitted campaign and content needs by line of business.

Step 2

Creative Review

I art directed the work, keeping brand voice and color system consistent.

Step 3

Approval

Sign-off from the relevant Senior Marketing Director before anything shipped.

03 / Corporate Communications

Enriching Our Industry

One corporate campaign I art directed spotlighted Caliber's leadership team — made up mostly of women — sharing their stories in their own words. I used one of the brand's palette colors, pink, to give the series a distinct, consistent look, then carried that same treatment across social, a landing page and an email send.

Every touchpoint — the landing page, the email, and each teammate feature — kept the same pink palette, quote treatment and photography style, so the campaign read as one connected story wherever people encountered it.

Training Brand Guidelines: TAP & Changing Lanes

Internal Guidelines Booklet

Partnered with the VP of Marketing to document how the Collision, Auto Glass and ProTech brands should be applied across Caliber's technician-training programs.
