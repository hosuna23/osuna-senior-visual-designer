Music Streaming App

Foundational UX research

Who streams, where, and what would make them switch? A survey, competitive scan and personas that pointed the MVP toward offline reliability, better discovery and simple pricing.

Role
UX researcher
Year
Spring 2025
Program
UT Austin UI/UX Designer certification
Methods
Survey, competitive scan, personas, empathy maps
Fielded
Two weeks, social posts and DMs
Tools
Google Forms, Figma
17Participants, across Texas, California and Mexico
20Questions in 7 sections
88%Listen daily, mostly on mobile
5Services in the competitive scan
Objective

Understand behaviors, pain points and switching triggers.

The findings would inform MVP features, pricing direction and the discovery and playlist strategy. Browse the research slides below; double-click for full screen.

View the survey Research slides in Figma
Top insights

Price and interruptions decide who stays.

Participants skewed 18 to 44 and 65% female. They listen on the move: commuting, at work and at the gym. That context explains almost every frustration they described.

What would make you switch?
Lower price47%
Uninterrupted access29%
How they listen
Daily listeners, mostly mobile88%
Often build their own playlists~30%

Discovery & playlists

Most lean on app-curated playlists. Better recommendations and themed mixes matter more than creation tools.

Interruptions & access

Ads and unreliable offline playback are the biggest breaking points.

Switching triggers

Price and uninterrupted access dominate: tiered plans and strong offline.

Devices & contexts

Car and gym first: hands-free controls and downloaded playback.

Personas
O

Oscar West

Tech-savvy DJ and engineer

Context
Time-constrained; uses music to focus and unwind
Needs
Quick mood presets, daily auto-mixes, repeat and artist-only modes
Pain
Interruptions, weak offline, repetitive recommendations
Opportunity
DJ-style curated sets; focus tracks surfaced by context
M

Mia Cruz

Culinary student on the go

Context
Balancing school and work; music for energy and culture
Needs
Hands-free controls, downloaded playlists, genre discovery by region
Pain
Time pressure, hard-to-find artists, ads breaking flow
Opportunity
Creator-curated lists; muted “window” ads that don’t pause playback
Opportunities
  • “Sounds like…” AI-assisted recommendations and DJ-style sets
  • Built-in recognition to capture what’s playing and add it to a playlist
  • Clearer playlist controls: repeat, skip, visible track length, artist-only mode
  • An opt-in social layer to follow friends and creators without friction
Impact

The MVP focused on offline reliability, recommendation quality and a simple tiered plan: a roadmap built around commute, work and gym.
