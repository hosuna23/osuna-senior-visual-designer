ADI Global Distribution

Make complex tasks easier to understand.

An instructional video that walks customers through ordering, shipping options and invoices on the ADI website. I handled the edit, motion graphics and audio; the script and AI-generated voiceover were supplied with the digital content team.

Shipping Options · ADIWatch on YouTube ↗

A screen-led tutorial: each step is shown, highlighted and named before the viewer has to act.

The brief

Help customers complete ordering, shipping and invoice tasks on their own, without calling support.

Motion with a job

Zooms, callouts and cursor highlights point to exactly one thing at a time, so the screen never competes with the voiceover.

Built from the brand

Titles and lower thirds use ADI type and color, built in Illustrator and animated in After Effects.

My role

Editing, motion graphics and audio production in Premiere Pro, After Effects and Illustrator.

How I work

Editing is part of the design system.

I treat a video like any other design deliverable: a clear goal, a structure people can follow, brand-consistent craft, and a review loop before it ships.

1

2

3

Edit

Rough cut for story and pacing first.

4

Motion & sound

Graphics, music, mix and color.

5

Deliver

Review, captions and exports for each channel.
