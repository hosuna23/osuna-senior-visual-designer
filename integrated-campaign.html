ADI Free Shipping

One message, adapted to every placement

A limited-time promotion to grow sales and memberships on the ADI digital store. One truck-and-box mark gave the offer a signature that stayed recognizable from a phone banner to a building-sized sign.

The brief

Make a shipping offer feel like news.

Free ground shipping on online orders of $100 or more, for a limited time. Contractors already knew ADI’s branches; the goal was to move more of those orders online and grow digital-store memberships before the year-end deadline.

Audience

Busy security and AV installers who skim on a phone between jobs, and walk past in-branch signage.

Constraint

The offer had to read in under two seconds on a 320-pixel banner and from across a parking lot.

My contribution

The campaign concept and mark, then every application: email, web banners, marketing tiles, mobile app banners, poster and digital signage.

A visual shorthand

Truck plus box equals the offer.

Two universal icons, combined into a single badge with speed lines and a stamped banner. Because the mark carries the meaning, every layout could shrink the copy down to the essentials.

Where every pixel counts

In the smallest placements the mark carries the message alone, with the threshold as the only supporting line.

  • Email header. Mark plus one line, so it survives image-blocking with live text below.
  • Marketing tiles. Square crops for category pages and the cart, mark centered.
